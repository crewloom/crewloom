# Platform notes

Sizes are the checker's accepted ranges, not guarantees. Re-check in the platform's own help if a page looks cropped.

| Platform | Logo | Cover | Where it is edited |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn Page | Square, at least 300x300 | Ratio about 4:1 (1584x396) | Admin view, Edit page; Overview is on the Details tab |
| Facebook Page | Square, at least 170x170 (shown as a circle) | Ratio about 2.6:1 (820x312 or larger) | Switch into the Page profile first, then the cover and photo menus |
| X | Square, at least 400x400 | Ratio about 3:1 (1500x500) | Owner creates the account; the agent edits only an existing signed-in one |

## Rules that came from real runs

- **Create from the owner's profile.** Pages are created under the signed-in person. Never create a new personal account.
- **Slugs.** A LinkedIn address may already exist. Report it and propose another; do not claim the preferred one.
- **Facebook page mode.** Editing requires switching into the Page profile. The username lives in Settings, and Facebook asks the owner to re-enter the password. That step is the owner's.
- **Popup windows.** Settings that open a new window are outside the browser tool's reach. Hand off the exact values.
- **Premium prompts.** Dismiss upsells. Never start a trial or purchase.
- **Link previews.** A link card pushes later controls down. Remove the card or re-screenshot before clicking.
- **Threads post slowly.** After submitting, wait and check the profile counter. Do not resubmit on a blank screen.
- **Cover text safe zone.** Keep text inside the left 70% so avatar overlays do not cover it.
- **Language.** Post in the language the owner chose for that channel only.
